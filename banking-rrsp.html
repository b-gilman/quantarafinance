◈ Key fact

RRSP contributions reduce your taxable income dollar-for-dollar. A $10,000 contribution in a 40% tax bracket effectively costs you only $6,000 after the tax refund. Growth is tax-deferred until withdrawal — ideally in retirement when your income (and tax rate) is lower.
